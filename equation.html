Start at the top: everything a company owns is paid for by lenders (liabilities) or by shareholders (equity). Equity splits into what shareholders put in and what the company earned and kept.

Assets = Liabilities + Common Stock + (Revenue − Expenses − Dividends)

Remember

  • Revenue makes retained earnings go up.
  • Expenses and dividends make it go down.
  • Dividends are not an expense. They're profit handed back to shareholders, so they skip the income statement and come straight out of retained earnings.
  • This page treats the bakery as brand new, so retained earnings started at $0. For an older company, you'd start from last period's retained earnings and add this period's revenue − expenses − dividends.
