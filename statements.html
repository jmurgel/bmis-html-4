📒 Your account balances

Use this list for all three statements. These are every account's balance, in no particular order. Decide where each one belongs. Revenue and expenses go on the income statement. Beginning retained earnings and dividends go on the statement of retained earnings. Assets, liabilities and common stock go on the balance sheet.

Step 3 · Balance sheet

Cheat sheet

  • Income statement = a movie of one period. Revenue − Expenses = Net income.
  • Balance sheet = a snapshot of one day. Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity.
  • Statement of retained earnings: Beginning retained earnings + Net income − Dividends = Ending retained earnings.
  • Shareholders' equity = common stock + ending retained earnings.
  • Dividends are not an expense, so they never go on the income statement.
  • Order matters: income statement → statement of retained earnings → balance sheet. Each one hands a number to the next.
