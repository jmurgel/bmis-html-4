Step 1 · The account balances

These are every account's ending balance, in no particular order. Decide where each one belongs. Revenue and expenses go on the income statement. Assets, liabilities and capital go on the balance sheet.

Step 3 · Balance sheet

Cheat sheet

  • Income statement = a movie of one period. Revenue − Expenses = Net income.
  • Balance sheet = a snapshot of one day. Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
  • Equity on the balance sheet = owner's capital + net income (minus any withdrawals).
  • Order matters: do the income statement first, because the balance sheet needs its net income.
