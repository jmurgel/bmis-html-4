Story: Maya & Zoe's Bracelet Business

Two best friends, one week, five transactions, and a sparkly notebook that always balances. 💖

Home See Practice Statements Story Equation

Monday: The piggy banks 🐷

Best friends Maya and Zoe each put $25 from their savings into a glittery cash box labeled "Maya & Zoe Beads." Maya's Aunt Rosa, a CPA who runs her own firm, hands them a pink notebook. "Every time money moves," she says, "write down what changed and where it came from. Both sides, every time."

They write: Cash +$50 = Owners' Equity +$50. "It's all ours," Zoe grins. "That's exactly what equity means," says Aunt Rosa.

Tuesday: A loan from Mom 💸

Maya's mom lends them $20 for a bead organizer, as long as they pay her back by Friday. Their cash grows, but now they owe someone.

Cash +$20 = Owed to Mom +$20. "That's a liability," Aunt Rosa says. "It's money you have, but it isn't all yours."

Wednesday: Bead shopping 🛍️

They spend $30 on beads, charms and stretchy string. Their cash shrinks, but they're not poorer: they now own supplies.

Supplies +$30, Cash −$30. "You traded one asset for another," Aunt Rosa says. "Your total didn't change."

Thursday: The big sale ✨

The whole dance team orders friendship bracelets for their competition and pays $60. Making them uses up $20 of supplies.

Cash +$60 → Revenue +$60.
Supplies −$20 → Expense −$20.

"Revenue makes your equity grow," says Aunt Rosa, "and expenses shrink it. What's left over, $40, is your net income."

Friday: Paying Mom back 💕

Zoe hands Maya's mom her $20. Cash −$20, Owed to Mom −$20.

The girls add up their notebook:

Cash$80
Supplies$10
Total assets$90
Liabilities$0
Equity ($50 invested + $40 net income)$90
Liabilities + equity$90

"It balances!" they shout together. Aunt Rosa smiles. "It always will, if you write down both sides. Welcome to running a business, ladies."

What Maya & Zoe learned

  • Assets = Liabilities + Equity, after every transaction.
  • Borrowing increases assets and liabilities.
  • Buying supplies with cash is a swap. Totals don't change.
  • Revenue − Expenses = Net income, which grows equity.
