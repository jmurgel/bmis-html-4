Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Whether you dream of owning a bakery, a boutique or a beauty brand, every business you'll ever run rests on this one line. Whatever a business owns was paid for either by someone it owes or by its owners. Every transaction changes at least two things, and the two sides always stay balanced.

Key terms

  • Assets: things the business owns, such as cash, inventory, equipment, and accounts receivable (money customers owe you).
  • Liabilities: what the business owes, such as loans, accounts payable (bills you haven't paid yet), and unearned revenue.
  • Equity: the owners' claim. It grows with owner investment and revenue, and it shrinks with expenses and withdrawals or dividends.
  • Debit (Dr): the left side of an account. Credit (Cr): the right side. In every journal entry, total debits = total credits.
  • DEALER: a memory trick. Dividends, Expenses, Assets go up with a debit. Liabilities, Equity, Revenue go up with a credit.
